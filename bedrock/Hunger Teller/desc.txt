Hunger Teller Mod
Never guess or waste your food resources again. Hunger Teller is an essential quality-of-life utility that takes the guesswork out of survival mechanics by displaying precise hunger and saturation metrics directly on your interface.

Instead of memorizing values or blindly eating expensive food items, this pack gives you an instant, visual preview of exactly how much value a food item provides before you even take a bite.

Key Features

Visual Hunger Previews: Automatically displays clean hunger bar indicators right next to the item name, showing you exactly how many drumsticks the food item will restore.

Full Inventory Integration: Works seamlessly inside your standard inventory screen, hotbar selections, chest storage systems, and crafting menus.

100% Achievement Safe: Built natively as an interface utility. Activating this pack will not disable your world achievements or flag your survival progress.
